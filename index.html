Digital Circuits Project

Interactive tools for understanding digital logic and circuit design

Welcome

This project explores the fundamentals of digital circuits through three interactive simulators. Each tool covers a different aspect of digital logic design — from Boolean algebra to combinational circuits to display decoding.

⚡ 1

Boolean Logic Solver

Truth tables, Boolean expressions, minterms, maxterms, Karnaugh maps, and logic circuit diagrams. Supports AND, OR, NOT, NAND, NOR, and XOR gates.

 🔧 2

Digital Circuits Simulator

Adders, subtractors, and multiplexers with live input toggling, interactive circuit diagrams, truth tables, and Boolean expressions.

 🔢 3

7-Segment Display Simulator

BCD-to-7-segment decoder with interactive mode, decade counter, truth tables, Karnaugh maps, and circuit diagrams.
